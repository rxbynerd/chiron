Report requirements:

- Open with a `#` title naming the topic, followed by an executive
  summary of at most three paragraphs.
- Organise the findings under `##` section headings. Use tables to
  compare options or present data series; use bulleted lists sparingly.
- Keep the tone precise and neutral. Prefer concrete figures with their
  units and dates over vague quantifiers, and name the primary source

  disagree, present the disagreement rather than papering over it.
- Close with a `## Conclusion` section summarising what is established,
  what remains uncertain, and what further research would resolve the
  uncertainty.
